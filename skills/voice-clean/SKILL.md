# Voice clean-up

## When to use
Laptop or phone microphones, echoey rooms, interviews and voice notes.

## Procedure
1. Apply voice-focused cleanup and presence boost.
2. If the recording also has a music bed, prefer @noise-removal first (it isolates the voice).

## Verification
Output must contain audible speech; silent output stops the chain.
